How a project runs

From a sketch to a finished part

Every job goes through the same four stages. You approve the design before anything is printed.

  1. 1

    Brief & sketch

    You send an idea, a photo, a drawing or a broken part. We agree on size, material and quantity.

    Input · photo, PDF, sample
  2. 2

    CAD model

    Our design team models the part and shares renders for your approval.

    STEP · STL · OBJ
  3. 3

    Print

    We pick the process and layer height for the job: fine detail or strength.

    Layer 0.08–0.28 mm · FDM & resin
  4. 4

    Finish & ship

    Sanding, priming, painting and assembly, then packed and shipped.

    Pan-India · export
